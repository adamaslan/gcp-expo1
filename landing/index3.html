AI council

One question, multiple time horizons, explicit disagreement.

The council layer is the product's difference: it does not just summarize. It forces the short-term and long-term views to cite data, name invalidation points, and reconcile conflicts before a user acts.

Short-term council

For tactical trades, this council is built around speed, range, catalyst timing, and technical invalidation.

1 dayNext-session momentum and range.
2-5 daysSwing setup and catalyst timing.
1-4 weeksTrend strength and regime quality.
30-60 daysDurability, volatility, and macro risk.

Long-term council

For allocation decisions, this council stretches the question across earnings, trend, cycle, and structural horizons.

2-3 monthsTactical and earnings-cycle read.
6-12 monthsSector rotation and trend regime.
1-3 yearsBusiness cycle and macro phase.
3-5 yearsStructural shift or cyclical noise.

Agreement synthesis

When both councils answer, the synthesis layer weights conflicts and produces an execution plan tied to a primary horizon.

  • Names the horizons that agree.
  • Identifies the exact conflict.
  • Turns disagreement into sizing logic.

Example exchange

Should I add NVDA here or wait for a pullback?
Short-term: wait for ATR-defined entry. Long-term: add gradually if semis remain leadership. Conflict: price extension versus earnings durability.
Execution: starter size now, second tranche only if breadth confirms and RSI cools without breaking trend.
