AI Council

Six agents. Two parallel tracks.

No single model makes the call. Every ticker runs through a Swing Council (short-term) and a Growth Council (long-term) simultaneously — each with two specialized agents deliberating in sequence before issuing a verdict.

⚡ Parallel execution — both tracks run simultaneously per ticker

Each track runs agents A→B in series internally. The tracks themselves are independent.

Track A · Swing Council
Short-Term Edge
1–5 day horizon
A1
Swing Discovery Agent
Deterministic feature scorer

Runs all 13 signal families through a weighted scoring algorithm. Produces a discovery score, directional call (long/short/neutral), and a prioritized list of supporting and counter evidence.

bollinger rsi macd volume correlation regime alignment sector_rel breadth options_sentiment vix_term cross_asset
A1 output feeds A2
A2
Swing Critic Agent
Rule-based decision refinement

Applies risk penalties, stale-data flags, and reward/risk estimation to A1's raw score. Issues entry zones, invalidation levels, and R:R ratios. Verdict: reject · needs_review · pass · watch · mutate

risk penalties stale data entry zone invalidation R:R estimate
Track A Output — FinalSwingDecision
decision total_score discovery_score critique_score risk_flags blockers supporting_evidence counter_evidence
Track B · Growth Council
Long-Term Quality
2–5 year horizon
B1
Growth Quality Agent
Fundamental multi-dimension scorer

Scores 8 fundamental dimensions from income statements, balance sheets, and cash flow data. Any hard reject flag (e.g. negative ROIC trend, deteriorating balance sheet) immediately overrides the numeric score.

revenue growth earnings quality ROIC trend moat durability capital allocation mgmt alignment valuation balance sheet
B1 output feeds B2
B2
Growth Tax Risk Agent
Tax & risk penalty applier

Adjusts B1's quality score for tax-related risks, stale fundamental data, and structural risk flags. Issues a direction: accumulate · hold · avoid

tax risk stale fundamentals hard rejects direction
Track B Output — FinalGrowthDecision
decision total_score quality_score tax_risk_score direction hard_rejects risk_flags

Macro Agent

Synthesizes cross-asset signals, yield curve shape, DXY trend, credit spreads, and HMM regime state into a macro backdrop that both councils reference before scoring. A risk_off regime increases penalty weights across all swing signals.

Market Overview Agent

Provides aggregate market health: breadth oscillators (Zweig thrust, McClellan), advance/decline ratios, new highs/lows, and overall market momentum. Used to assess whether individual signals are swimming with or against the tide.
