The selling point

Robust signals. Proprietary data. Say it everywhere.

The strongest claim NWF can make is the one competitors can't copy in a weekend: the verdict layer is built from many independent reads, not one loud indicator, and every verdict it has ever issued is logged into a dataset nobody else has.

Robustness by architecture

The engine is robust the way a bridge is robust — any single leg can fail and the read still holds.

  • 138 signal outputs compressed from 380+ data points per symbol
  • Five independent evidence families: technical, volatility, macro regime, sector rotation, events
  • Every verdict ships with confidence, horizon fit, and an explicit invalidation level
  • Council answers must cite exact data and name the conflict before a user acts

The proprietary dataset

Three corpora accumulate with every market day and every user — none are purchasable.

  • Verdict ledger: append-only Hold/Fold + signal history with timestamps → publishable rolling hit-rates
  • Council corpus: horizon-tagged reasoning chains (T1/T2) grounded in real indicator readings
  • Regime archive: daily macro-pulse scores forming a labeled regime timeline

The moat sentence

"Every NWF verdict is built from five independent evidence families and logged to an append-only track record — so you can check our hit-rate before you trust our next call."

Use this (or a tightened cut of it) on the landing hero, the pricing page, the App Store listing, and slide 2 of the deck. It converts the ML claim from marketing into data — VC move 4 in gcp3-mobile-robust-50.md.

Voice — unchanged, enforced

Say / never say.

Say

  • "Advanced AI-native financial tools" (category)
  • Concrete numbers: 138 signals, 380+ data points, 6 council modes
  • "Unit economics by architecture", "invalidation level", "evidence families"
  • "Create your account" / "Open the app" / "Check our record"

Never say

  • GCP Intelligence, GCP3, Signal Garden (dead internal names)
  • "AI-powered" as a standalone claim — say what the AI does
  • "Revolutionary", "game-changing", "unlock the future"
  • "Join the waitlist" — the product is live
Litmus test for every new line of copy: does it survive a skeptic asking "prove it"? If the proof isn't in the product or the ledger, cut the line.
